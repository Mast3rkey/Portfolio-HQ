## Rationale

**Why `PI-####`, not a new prefix.** This is Company Intelligence freshness/currency-maintenance content (`category: portfolio_intelligence`), matching the `PI-0003`/`PI-0005`/`PI-0007`/`PI-0009`/`PI-0018`/`PI-0020`/`PI-0022`/`PI-0023`-`PI-0038` historical series precedent for evidence-refresh and coverage work — not a new decision-prefix domain (`REL-####`/`TIER-####`/`LADDER-####`/`CHART-####` each covers a genuinely new record type or research charter this filing does not touch).

**Why this filing does not flip `PI-0038`'s own status.** This repository's own established two-step acceptance-recording convention (`TIER-0002`'s treatment of `TIER-0001`, `REL-0003` through `REL-0006`'s treatment of the prior `REL-####` filing, `CHART-0001`/`CHART-0002`'s own dedicated acceptance-recording bullets) consistently leaves a confirmed decision's own frontmatter `status` field untouched by the filing that confirms its merge — the status transition is its own separate, later, explicit Lane G action, not folded automatically into an unrelated filing's Lane M synchronization. This filing follows that same discipline for `PI-0038`.

**Why 13 edits and not 27, and why LLY/RKLB were not edited despite being flagged.** The governing task packet is explicit: "Update a record only where a verified material event makes it stale, misleading, or incomplete... Update only records classified UPDATE REQUIRED." LLY and RKLB are UNRESOLVED, not UPDATE REQUIRED — LLY's actual Q2 2026 results could not be confirmed released as of this session's research; RKLB's disclosed gaps were re-tested and confirmed still open, which is itself new information (a persisting gap, not a new material fact) but does not meet the UPDATE REQUIRED bar. Editing either would mean asserting a fact this session could not verify.

**Why the `next_due` arithmetic correction was made for TSLA only, not the other four (`ICE`/`SPGI`/`WM`/`RKLB`) or two (`SNPS`/`GNRC`) records sharing the same class of error.** TSLA was already being edited for a confirmed material reason (the robotaxi-geography correction); fixing an adjacent, unambiguous arithmetic error in the same `review:` block on the same file in the same edit pass is a mechanical correction, not new scope. Making the identical fix to five records this filing was not otherwise touching would exceed the "update only UPDATE REQUIRED records" boundary — disclosed instead as a non-actioned observation in the supporting artifact for a future session.
