### OPS-0008 §2 disclosure (added 2026-08-05, following independent review — a gap in this filing's original text, not a new fact)

`OPS-0008` §2 ("Source-readiness gate — mandatory stop-before-drafting, with standing evidence-recovery pre-authorization") requires that when direct primary-source access is blocked for one or more companies, **the implementing session must stop before drafting substantive economic content for those companies** — it may not proceed on secondary/WebSearch-only evidence and rely on a later correction pass, and it has a standing, pre-authorized remedy available (engaging an eligible independent reviewer per `OPS-0007` §1 to perform a primary-source evidence-recovery audit before resuming drafting). This filing's original text listed `OPS-0008` in `related_decisions` but **never addressed §2 at all**, and the six research sessions that produced `SNPS`/`ICE`/`SPGI`/`WM`/`RKLB`/`TSLA` did not stop when WebFetch was found blocked — each proceeded directly to drafting on WebSearch-only evidence, and none engaged §2's standing evidence-recovery method before this bounded correction pass.

**Why this filing does not treat that omission as disqualifying, while still disclosing it plainly rather than smoothing it over**: `OPS-0008` textually scopes itself to "future Milestone 3 batches" (its own §1 default-wave-size language and its title, "Research Wave Protocol v1," are both written against the Milestone 3 batch-research pattern this filing explicitly is not — see "Why these six, why now" above, which states this is a Milestone-6-preparation unit, not a Milestone 3 batch). `PI-0036` (GNRC/RTX) — the immediately preceding governance precedent for this exact non-Milestone-3, gated/administrative-pairing category — already proceeded on the same snippet-only sourcing pattern without invoking §2, and was accepted. This filing is consistent with, not a new departure from, that actual repository practice. This is a textual-scope argument, not a claim that §2's underlying purpose (don't draft consequential content on unverified secondary sourcing without at least considering the standing recovery option) is irrelevant here — it plainly is relevant, which is why the bounded correction below exists.

**What this filing does instead, in this same bounded correction pass**: rather than retroactively invoking §2's full independent-reviewer evidence-recovery audit (a heavier process than this correction's own authorized scope permits), this session performed **narrow, targeted WebSearch-only re-verification** of each disputed claim identified by independent review — attempting the cited primary source (SEC filing, company investor-relations release, FTC record) first, confirming WebFetch remains blocked on every domain attempted on a fresh 2026-08-05 attempt (sec.gov, ftc.gov, iridium.com, and others), and either narrowing/correcting the claim with the best WebSearch-synthesized evidence available or explicitly flagging what remains unresolved. **This is explicitly not equivalent to §2's own evidence-recovery method or to a direct primary-source read** — it is the same access-constrained research method already disclosed throughout all six records, applied a second time, more narrowly, to the specific claims independent review flagged. No claim corrected in this pass should be read as now primary-source-verified.

## Rationale

**Why `PI-####`, not a new prefix.** This is Company Intelligence coverage-completion content (`category: portfolio_intelligence`), matching the `PI-0003`/`PI-0005`/`PI-0007`/`PI-0009`/`PI-0023`-`PI-0037` historical series precedent, most directly `PI-0036` (the GNRC/RTX gated-adjacent wave) — not workstream-register mechanics (`OPS-####`).

**Why one filing covering six structurally unrelated companies is proper here.** `OPS-0008` §1's common-economic-mechanism requirement governs when multiple companies are presented as one coherent economic-thesis batch — it does not forbid a single governance filing from authorizing multiple independent research units, as `PI-0032` (six independent units, no shared mechanism) and `PI-0036` (two independent units, no shared mechanism) already established. This filing follows that precedent at six-company scale: SNPS (EDA software), ICE (financial exchanges/data), SPGI (credit ratings/data/indices), WM (waste management), RKLB (space launch/manufacturing), and TSLA (EVs/energy/autonomy) share no genuine economic mechanism, and this filing makes no such claim — their only shared attribute is identical portfolio-governance status (all six gated under `PHQ-2026-01`, no prior Intelligence coverage).

**Why a narrow `PI-0033` supersession for RKLB/TSLA only, and none needed for the other four.** RKLB and TSLA carry a pre-existing, literal `PI-0033` deferral whose trigger has not occurred — stating the supersession explicitly, bounded to research-authorization scope, is more transparent than silently reinterpreting the trigger. SNPS, ICE, SPGI, and WM carry no such prior disposition to supersede; `PI-0035` already recorded them as newly dispositioned with research left unauthorized until a filing like this one occurs.

**Why this is not a Milestone 3 reopening.** `PI-0037`'s own completion determination is unedited and unreopened by this filing — it evaluated `PI-0031` §K's seven criteria as they were actually written, which do not require every gated name to carry Company Intelligence coverage, only an explicit disposition (which the gate itself supplies). This filing is a separate, forward-looking authorization justified by Milestone 6 preparation, not a claim that Milestone 3's determination needs correction.

**Why the six-step roadmap is recorded now, in this same filing, rather than a separate PR.** Per explicit principal instruction: folding the roadmap synchronization into this filing's own PR avoids a separate planning-only PR and keeps the ordered sequence visible in the same place as the unit that satisfies its first step.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Wait for each of RKLB's/TSLA's own independent `PI-0033` trigger to occur naturally before researching them, researching the other four separately.** Rejected — this would leave the roster's gated-name coverage permanently split and inconsistent, and the principal explicitly authorized one coherent six-name unit.
- **Fabricate a shared economic mechanism across the six to satisfy `OPS-0008` §1's default bundling standard.** Rejected on the merits and on the `PI-0032`/`PI-0036` precedent — the six do not share one genuine mechanism, and inventing one would misstate every company's actual business.
- **Treat this filing as reopening or amending `PI-0031` §K's Milestone 3 completion criteria.** Rejected — Milestone 3 is already complete (`PI-0037`) under criteria that do not require this coverage; this filing is additive, forward-looking preparation for Milestone 6, not a correction.
- **Defer recording the six-step pre-Milestone-6 roadmap until each step is separately authorized.** Rejected by explicit principal instruction — the ordered sequence itself, including which steps remain unauthorized, is meant to be durably recorded now so a future session (or the principal) can see the full plan at a glance, without any step beyond step 1 being treated as authorized to execute.
- **File a separate roadmap-only PR.** Rejected by explicit principal instruction — fold into this same filing.
