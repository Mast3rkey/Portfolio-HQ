### Why GNRC and RTX, why now

`PI-0031` §K's Milestone 3 completion criterion 4 requires every remaining uncovered canonical-roster company to be covered, explicitly deferred with rationale, or reassigned. `PI-0033` supplied that rationale for GNRC and RTX (among fourteen names) by recording a deferral, not a permanent exclusion — each carries its own reopening trigger, restated above. The principal has now determined, as a matter of portfolio governance rather than of either company's own disclosed facts, that GNRC and RTX are the only two canonical equity destinations simultaneously **held, targeted, ungated, and missing Company Intelligence coverage** — every other uncovered or previously-deferred name is either already covered (WDC), gated (SNPS, ICE, SPGI, WM, RKLB, TSLA), or not part of this wave's authorization for any other reason. This is a portfolio-governance-status determination, independently verified against live repository state above, not a claim that GNRC's and RTX's underlying businesses have anything in common.

### Administrative wave, not economic coherence

`OPS-0008` §1's default wave-bundling standard requires a documented **common economic mechanism** — not merely a shared sector, tier, or governance status — before multiple companies are researched together as one coherent batch (Batches 1-9 each named a real shared driver: semis equipment, memory, compute/networking, power infrastructure, hyperscaler AI infrastructure, financial infrastructure, biopharmaceuticals, enterprise software/cybersecurity, and the `oil` cluster). **GNRC and RTX do not share one genuine economic mechanism.** GNRC is a backup-power-generation and energy-technology manufacturer whose core revenue driver is residential/commercial power-outage and grid-resilience demand (with an emerging, minority, contractually-disclosed data-center/hyperscale C&I leg — see `intelligence/companies/GNRC.md` §13). RTX is an aerospace/defense conglomerate (Collins Aerospace, Pratt & Whitney, Raytheon) whose drivers are commercial-aerospace aftermarket cycles, U.S. and allied defense procurement, and program/engine-specific risk. These are not the same mechanism, not adjacent mechanisms, and this decision does not claim otherwise.

**Precedent for bundling without a shared mechanism: `PI-0032`.** That decision authorized six independent governed-holding research units (WDC, CEG, BRK.B, WMT, MLM, AAPL) in one filing, "each treated as its own independent unit rather than a fabricated shared-mechanism batch," explicitly because "no genuine common economic driver exists among a utility, an insurance/holding conglomerate, a retailer, a materials producer, and a consumer-tech company." This filing follows that exact precedent, at a smaller scale (two names, not six): GNRC and RTX are each researched, evidenced, and drafted as its own independent unit, not as a two-company economic-mechanism batch. Their bundling into one governance filing and one implementation PR is justified solely by identical portfolio-governance status (held, targeted, ungated, uncovered) and execution efficiency (one branch, one PR, one validation pass) — never by, and never described elsewhere in either company's record as, a shared business thesis.

## Rationale

**Why `PI-####`, not a new prefix.** This is Company Intelligence coverage-disposition-and-completion content (`category: portfolio_intelligence`), matching the `PI-0003`/`PI-0005`/`PI-0007`/`PI-0009`/`PI-0023`-`PI-0033` historical series precedent — not workstream-register mechanics (`OPS-####`) and not a numerical research charter (`MARGIN-####`/`LADDER-####`).

**Why one filing covering two structurally unrelated companies is proper here, not a violation of `OPS-0008` §1.** `OPS-0008` §1's common-economic-mechanism requirement governs when multiple companies are presented as one coherent economic-thesis batch — it does not forbid a single governance filing from authorizing multiple independent research units, as `PI-0032` already established at six-company scale. This filing follows that precedent explicitly, at two-company scale, and repeatedly states (here, and in each company's own record) that no shared mechanism claim is being made.

**Why exactly GNRC and RTX, and no third name.** The preflight section above is this decision's own evidentiary basis: of the roster's canonical, currently-held equity destinations, GNRC and RTX are the only two simultaneously held, targeted, ungated, and uncovered. Every alternative candidate is excluded on an independently-verified, stated basis (WDC: covered; SNPS/ICE/SPGI/WM/RKLB/TSLA: gated).

**Why a narrow `PI-0033` supersession, not a claim that no supersession is needed.** Reading `PI-0033`'s reopening triggers literally, neither name's trigger has occurred; treating this filing as though a trigger had occurred without saying so would misstate the record. Stating the supersession explicitly, and bounding it to research-authorization scope only, preserves `PI-0033`'s controlling force for every other name it addresses and is more transparent to a future reader than silently reinterpreting "a future coherent research wave" to cover an administrative pairing it plainly did not contemplate.

**Why one PR, not `OPS-0008`'s default two-PR (governance-then-implementation) lifecycle.** `OPS-0008` §4's default two-PR structure exists to gate a research batch's actual drafting behind the governance filing's own independent review and merge. The principal's authorization for this specific bounded unit explicitly permits this session to perform live repository preflight, retrieve and inspect primary sources, draft both companies' complete records, update freshness registries, create this governing decision, synchronize `WS-0005`, validate, and open one draft PR — all within one implementation lane, with independent review and principal acceptance still required before merge (identical end-state gate to every prior batch; only the pre-merge single-session bounded-drafting authorization differs, and only for this filing). This is the same category of principal-directed narrowing `OPS-0009`'s lane system already anticipates for a bounded unit smaller than a full committee-review cycle.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Wait for each ticker's own independent `PI-0033` trigger to occur naturally.** Rejected by explicit principal instruction — this could mean an indefinite, possibly permanent, absence of Company Intelligence coverage for two currently-held, targeted, ungated positions, for no reason connected to either company's actual materiality or evidence availability.
- **Fabricate a shared "power/defense" or "industrial" economic mechanism to satisfy `OPS-0008` §1's default bundling standard.** Rejected on the merits and per the principal's own explicit instruction (item 4) — GNRC and RTX do not share one genuine economic mechanism, and inventing one would misstate both companies' actual businesses and would corrupt the evidentiary integrity `OPS-0008` §1 exists to protect.
- **File two entirely separate governance decisions and two separate implementation PRs (one per company).** Rejected on efficiency grounds under the principal's explicit parallel-research-and-single-mutation-lane authorization — both companies are independently researched and independently drafted regardless; splitting the governance/implementation package into two PRs would double branch, PR, validation, and review overhead without changing either company's own evidentiary content, and both companies share identical portfolio-governance-status reasoning for inclusion, which a single filing states once rather than twice.
- **Treat this as an ordinary `PI-0033` reopening rather than an explicit supersession.** Rejected — see "PI-0033 narrow supersession" above; stating the true relationship (neither literal trigger occurred) is more transparent than an implicit reinterpretation.
- **Broaden this wave to a third or later name to reach `OPS-0008` §1's 5-6-company default size.** Rejected by explicit principal instruction — no third ticker is authorized, and `OPS-0031`/`PI-0031` §K precedent (CVX, filed alone) already establishes that a smaller, coherence-justified wave is proper when no larger genuinely coherent group exists; this filing's own justification is portfolio-governance status, not economic coherence, and adding a third name on no basis beyond reaching a numeric default would be exactly the kind of forced pairing `OPS-0008` §1 exists to prevent.
