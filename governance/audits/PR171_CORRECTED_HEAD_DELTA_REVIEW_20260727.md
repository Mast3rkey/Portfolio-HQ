## 6. Blocking finding F171-3 — capital-return funding claims exceed the evidence

The corrected records and comparison make causal funding claims including:

- Visa's capital-return program was “funded from operating cash flow, not external debt issuance”;
- Mastercard's capital return was “funded from operating cash flow”;
- both companies “fund large capital-return programs from operating cash flow without a disclosed
  material debt build.”

The evidence establishes operating cash flow, investment outlays, debt balances, repurchases, and
dividends in the same fiscal periods. It does not trace the source of each dollar used for capital
returns, establish that no opening cash was used, or establish a year-over-year debt change from the
figures retained in the record.

Report the facts in parallel. It is permissible to state that operating cash flow was large relative
to the disclosed investment outlays and capital returns. Do not assert a causal funding source or
absence of debt financing without direct evidence.

## 7. Blocking finding F171-4 — JPM's comparison mechanism is overgeneralized

The comparison says that the majority of JPM's revenue and risk outside Payments/Securities
Services is “balance-sheet-intermediation-specific,” listing net interest income, Investment
Banking, Markets, and Asset & Wealth Management together.

That collapses distinct mechanisms:

- deposit and lending net interest income is balance-sheet intermediation;
- Investment Banking is principally advisory and underwriting activity;
- Markets combines client intermediation, trading, and balance-sheet usage;
- Asset & Wealth Management is predominantly asset-management and servicing economics;
- Securities Services and Payments are additional fee/service businesses.

The central distinction from V/MA remains valid, but the corrected wording should describe JPM as
a **diversified regulated financial intermediary**, then separate deposit/lending, advisory,
markets, custody/servicing, payments, and asset-management mechanisms. Do not label every
non-network revenue line as balance-sheet intermediation.
