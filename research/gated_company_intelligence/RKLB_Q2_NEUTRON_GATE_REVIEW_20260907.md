## Technical-gate analysis

Management described "critical milestones" across assembly, integration, and
testing, but did not disclose an objective pass/fail event. The Form 10-Q says
qualification testing was ongoing, the Stage 1 tank was aligned with targeted
Q4 pad delivery, the end-of-year launch window was narrowing, and exact timing
depended on first-stage assembly and test outcomes.

Those statements describe progress, not completion. Treating the adjective
"critical" as gate satisfaction would let management choose the milestone
after the fact and would make the control non-falsifiable.

The minimum prospective checkpoint for the next review is therefore:

> Rocket Lab publicly confirms that the first-flight Stage 1 tank
> completed qualification and that the integrated first stage was delivered to
> Launch Complex 3, with any material test exceptions disclosed.

This is tied to unfinished first-stage testing and schedule risk, observable in issuer disclosure, and
defined before achievement. It is a review trigger, not automatic purchase
authority. The gate remains closed until a separate reviewed disposition.

## Capital quality and dilution

Rocket Lab's $2.388B of cash and marketable securities provides development
runway, but it was not generated by operations. First-half operating cash
outflow was $134.4M while gross ATM equity proceeds were $1.530B. Common shares
outstanding rose from 543.6M to 598.2M in six months, and Q3 guidance uses 641M
basic weighted-average shares including participating preferred shares.

The pending Iridium transaction further changes the capital structure. It uses
cash and Rocket Lab stock, requires multiple regulatory and shareholder
approvals, and is supported by a commitment for a $3.6B 364-day senior secured
bridge facility. Any future intrinsic-value model must be transaction-aware.

## Issuer claims versus analyst interpretation

Revenue, guidance, backlog, cash, transaction terms, and Neutron schedules are
issuer-reported. Derived margins, net cash, and valuation multiples are analyst
calculations from those inputs. The conclusion that the technical milestone is
unmet is an analyst interpretation based on the absence of a disclosed,
objective completion event.

## Gate disposition

Retain `cash_pending_clearance` and `allow_add: false`. Q2 financial evidence is
now available, but the prospectively defined technical checkpoint has not been
reported as completed. Do not change the accepted 0.50% target or any sleeve
assignment in this evidence-only unit.
