- ICE's July 30, 2026 Q2 release reports net revenue of $2.666 billion, GAAP diluted EPS of $1.69, GAAP operating income of $1.391 billion, and a 52% operating margin. Segment revenue was $1.464 billion for Exchanges, $645 million for Fixed Income and Data Services, and $557 million for Mortgage Technology.
- The same release reports unrestricted cash of $1.067 billion and debt of $19.846 billion at June 30, 2026. These values reconcile the previously approximate $1.1 billion and $19.8 billion figures.
- ICE's July 30 transaction release states a $167 cash price for MarketAxess, approximately $6.0 billion equity value and $5.7 billion enterprise value. It says the consideration will be financed with newly issued debt.
- The transaction release defines the expected starting gross leverage as 3.4x and the target as 3.0x or below within 18 to 24 months after close. It also states $100 million of annual run-rate expense synergies within three years and an expected first-half 2027 close, subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals.
- Market snapshot at 2026-09-03 14:47 UTC: $161.28 per share, approximately $91.3 billion market capitalization, 22.78x trailing P/E, and $7.08 trailing EPS.

### Issuer claims

- First-year adjusted-EPS accretion, the three-year synergy realization, and the 18-to-24-month deleveraging path are forward-looking issuer claims, not observed outcomes.

### Analyst interpretation

- Primary-source recovery closes the factual Q2 and announced-transaction-terms gap, but it does not close execution risk. Permanent debt pricing, regulatory clearance, integration delivery, and the actual post-close deleveraging path remain unobserved.
- The current $161.28 price is 33.1% above the $121.15 high end of the repository's prior illustrative range. That range was explicitly assumption-driven, stale, and corroborative-only, so this is a caution screen rather than a fair-value conclusion.
- Disposition: retain the ICE gate. The core business remains strong, but the remaining financing and integration uncertainties can still change the position-size decision.

### Issuer claims

- Guidance, integration benefits, and future margin expansion are issuer forecasts. They are not treated as realized evidence.

### Analyst interpretation

- Direct issuer and filing access closes the missing-Q2-package gap. Operations are resilient: adjusted EBITDA and free-cash-flow guidance were maintained despite the lower revenue range.
- The current $219.37 price is 36.4% above the $160.79 high end of the repository's prior illustrative range and carries a 31.75x trailing P/E. The prior range was assumption-driven and not peer-derived, so the gap is a valuation-warning screen rather than a precise fair-value estimate.
- A discount-rate-based DCF remains an honest abstention: this refresh did not obtain a governed, reproducible beta/equity-risk-premium/capital-structure set that satisfies the repository's valuation policy. Inventing those inputs would create false precision.
- Disposition: retain the WM gate. The Q2 evidence is now primary-source verified, but the current valuation does not offer enough robust evidence to clear the gate.
