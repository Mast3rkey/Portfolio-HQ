## Business summary

Pre-spin (FY2025 structure), SPGI operated five segments: Ratings, Market
Intelligence, Commodity Insights (renamed "S&P Global Energy" effective
2025-11-14), Indices (the S&P Dow Jones Indices joint venture -- CME
Group affiliates historically held 24.4%, McGraw-Hill/S&P Global side
~73%, Dow Jones & Company ~2.6%, per 2011-2012 JV-formation-era figures
with no 2026 confirmation found), and Mobility (spun off, see below).
[SPGI-C05]

FY2025 segment revenue mix (secondary aggregation, not independently
verified against the ARS/10-K directly): Market Intelligence ~$4.92B
(~37%), Ratings ~$4.72B (~36%), Indices ~$1.85B (~14%), Mobility ~$1.75B
(~13%, now spun off). **Corrected 2026-08-05 following independent
review**: an earlier draft of this record omitted the fifth segment,
**Commodity Insights** (renamed "S&P Global Energy" effective
2025-11-14, see below), from this list entirely -- the four segments
above summed to only ~$13.24B against the stated $15.336B FY2025 total,
a ~$2.1B (~13.7%) gap the reviewer correctly identified as an
unreconciled, incomplete reconciliation rather than a mere
rounding/percentage-labeling artifact. Targeted re-verification this
session found Commodity Insights FY2025 revenue of **~$1.95 billion
(~15.6% of total)**, cited by one secondary source (ycharts.com,
itself presumably derived from the 10-K, not directly opened this
session). Adding this figure closes the reconciliation to
~$15.19B against the $15.336B stated total (~99% accounted for, the
small residual most likely corporate/other/eliminations, not further
resolved this session) -- a materially better reconciliation than the
original four-segment list, though still not verified against the
primary 10-K text directly (WebFetch remained blocked on sec.gov on a
fresh attempt). FY2025 total revenue: **$15.336 billion**, +8% YoY;
GAAP net income $4.471B (+16%); GAAP diluted EPS $14.66 (+19%); adjusted
net income $5.441B (+11%).

Recast 2025 operating margins (post-Mobility-removal, per a 2026-07-06
pro forma release): Indices 68% GAAP / 70% adjusted; Ratings 64% GAAP /
65% adjusted; Energy 40% GAAP / 46% adjusted; Market Intelligence 19%
GAAP / 33% adjusted -- the large GAAP/adjusted gap specifically in Market

Market Intelligence is pursuing an AI-monetization push (ChatIQ, Document

## The Mobility separation -- central to the gate

**Mechanics**: record date 2026-06-15; when-issued trading 2026-06-26
through 2026-06-30; distribution effective 2026-07-01, 1-for-1 ratio,
fractional shares sold for cash. Resulting entity: **Mobility Global
Inc.** (NYSE: MBGL), began regular-way trading 2026-07-01; SPGI retains
no ownership interest. [SPGI-R01]

**Spinoff scope -- corrected/clarified 2026-08-05 following independent
review, which flagged this record's original framing as a possible
factual error.** An earlier draft of this record implicitly assumed a
Mobility-only separation without directly addressing whether other
segments (Commodity Insights, or a since-divested "Engineering
Solutions" business) were also part of it. Targeted re-verification this
session (WebSearch only -- WebFetch remained blocked on sec.gov and
ftc.gov on fresh attempts) found: (a) multiple independent secondary
sources (Yahoo Finance, The Motley Fool, StockTitan, CollisionWeek,
Repairer Driven News, Auto Remarketing) consistently describe Mobility
Global's portfolio as CARFAX, automotiveMastermind, Polk Automotive
Solutions, and Market Scan only; (b) one source states explicitly that
S&P Global continues as a provider of credit ratings, benchmarks,
analytics, and workflow solutions through four core remaining
businesses -- Market Intelligence, Ratings, **Commodity Insights**, and
S&P Dow Jones Indices -- indicating Commodity Insights was **not** part
of the Mobility separation; (c) "Engineering Solutions" was a
**distinct, unrelated, and much earlier transaction** -- S&P Global sold
its Engineering Solutions business (from the 2022 IHS Markit merger) to
KKR, completing 2023-05-02 for ~$750M after-tax proceeds; the business
now operates independently as Accuris, and this session found no
evidence connecting it to the 2026 Mobility separation in any way. **On
the specific, narrower evidence this session was able to recover, the
record's original Mobility-only framing appears better supported than a
broader Commodity-Insights-plus-Engineering-Solutions framing would be**
-- but this is still a WebSearch-synthesis conclusion, not a
primary-source-document read (the actual spinoff Form 10/8-K exhibit
was identified by search but not directly opened), and is presented here
as this session's own evidence-recovery finding, not as a settled fact
beyond dispute. A future session with working SEC access should confirm
directly against the Form 10/8-K text before this point is treated as
fully resolved either way.

**Accounting treatment -- the load-bearing fact for the gate's
comparability concern**: Mobility's results are included in SPGI's
consolidated financials only through 2026-06-30. **Beginning with Q3
2026, Mobility's historical results (all periods) will be reclassified
as discontinued operations.** This means that as of this record's
authoring date (2026-08-05), **SPGI has not yet reported a single
quarter under the clean, post-spin discontinued-operations
presentation** -- Q2 2026 (reported 2026-07-28) still included Mobility
operations through the June 30 cutoff and was explicitly a transition
quarter. The first quarter presenting Mobility as discontinued
operations across all comparative periods is Q3 2026, expected ~2026-10-
29. **This directly and literally matches the gate's own stated trigger
("one clean post-spin quarter") not yet having occurred** -- a factual
observation, not an opinion on timing.

**Pro forma disclosures already released ahead of the actual clean
quarter**: a 2026-07-06 press release provided recast FY2025 (all four
quarters) and Q1 2026 pro forma segment data with Mobility removed (Q1
2026 pro forma revenue: $3.717B), followed by an 8-K/A amendment.
**Transition Services Agreement (TSA)**: SPGI continues providing IT,
finance, HR, and other shared services to Mobility Global for a fee based
on fully-loaded costs, generally for up to 18 months post-distribution.
**Stranded costs** are characterized as having "a minor net impact,"
offset via TSA fee income (disclosed pro forma SG&A adjustments: -$6M
Q1 2026, -$9M per 2025 quarter, -$35M full-year 2025). **Funding
linkage**: SPGI received a $2B dividend from Mobility around the spin and
is using that plus ~$2B of planned H2 2026 new debt issuance to fund an
increased buyback target (see Balance sheet section below) -- the spin
generated a one-time cash inflow and is triggering fresh debt issuance,
not merely removing a segment.

**Market reaction context**: Mobility's 2026-05-12 inaugural Investor Day
was followed by a 4.15% SPGI stock drop the next day. Separately,
February 2026 FY2026 guidance (adjusted EPS $19.40-$19.65, below the
$19.96 consensus) triggered a 17% single-day premarket decline. As of
2026-08-03, SPGI closed at $416.25, down roughly 22-28.6% over the
trailing twelve months depending on measurement window, market cap
~$122.8B.

## Competitive position -- SPGI versus MSCI (direct comparison per the gate)

**Scale**: SPGI market cap ~$122.8B (2026-08-03) vs. MSCI ~$43B (an
earlier, likely somewhat stale snapshot) -- roughly 3x. **Growth**: MSCI
Q2 2026 revenue $867M (+12.2% YoY), adjusted EPS $4.94 (+18.5% YoY,
slight miss vs. $4.97 consensus); SPGI's closest analog (Market
Intelligence, +6%) trails MSCI's growth rate, while SPGI's consolidated
figure (+10-11%) is boosted by Ratings' cyclical transaction strength.
**Margins**: MSCI Q1 2026 operating margin 53.7%, net margin 47.7%; SPGI
Q1 2026 operating margin 48.0% GAAP (inflated by the one-time $175M
divestiture gain). By segment, SPGI's Indices (68-70%) and Ratings
(64-65%) run margins comparable to or above MSCI's blended figure, while
Market Intelligence (19-33%) and Energy (40-46%) pull the consolidated
average down -- a structural mix difference directly relevant to the
gate's comparison.

**Valuation multiples -- conflicting across sources, unresolved**: SPGI
trailing P/E cited as 27.85x, 24.7x, and 33.9x across sources; SPGI
EV/EBITDA cited as 11.56x, 18.07x, and 18.45x. MSCI trailing P/E ~34.43x
(falling from 35.86x to 30.01x across two 2026 snapshots); MSCI EV/EBITDA
~24.98x (vs. 22.49x at an earlier date). **Directionally, despite noisy
SPGI figures, MSCI appears to trade at a meaningfully higher
earnings/EBITDA multiple than SPGI on essentially every reading** --
consistent with MSCI's higher margin, more subscription-pure mix, and
the market's apparent discount on SPGI's post-spin uncertainty. **A
normalized, same-date, same-basis SPGI-versus-MSCI comparison -- exactly
what the gate's own text calls for -- was not achievable from sources
available this session.**

**Moat characteristics**: SPGI Ratings is part of the "Big Three" credit-
rating oligopoly (SPGI/Moody's/Fitch controlling ~95% combined per
secondary sources, individual split reported inconsistently) -- a
regulatory-license-driven moat. MSCI's Indices/Analytics/ESG moat is
switching-cost-driven (benchmarks and risk tools embedded in client
workflows). SPGI's Indices segment shares MSCI's AUM-linked-fee
mechanism. **Structural takeaway**: SPGI's moat is more heterogeneous
(a regulatory oligopoly plus an AUM-linked index franchise plus
lower-margin subscription data businesses) while MSCI's is more
concentrated (subscription/AUM-linked stickiness across index,
analytics, and ESG) -- precisely the kind of business-mix difference the
gate's comparison is meant to resolve.

## Investment thesis (descriptive, no recommendation)

**Concise statement:** SPGI's core Ratings/Indices/Market Intelligence/
Energy businesses are large, profitable, and structurally moated (Ratings
oligopoly, AUM-linked Indices franchise), with an exceptional
dividend-growth record (53 consecutive years); the currently gated status
reflects a real, independently-confirmed comparability gap opened by the
Mobility spin-off rather than a concern about underlying business
quality.

**Conditions that would support initiation**: a clean Q3 2026 report
presenting Mobility as discontinued operations across all comparative
periods; a reconciled bridge between the pre- and post-spin FY2026
guidance figures; a same-basis SPGI-vs-MSCI valuation comparison
constructed from primary-source data; confirmation of post-debt-issuance
leverage.

**Conditions that would argue for continued caution**: further guidance
volatility post-spin; leverage rising without a clear deleveraging path;
continued inability to reconcile SPGI's own valuation multiples across
data providers; a material TSA-related stranded-cost surprise.
