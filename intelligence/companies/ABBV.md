## Business summary

AbbVie is a diversified pharmaceutical company in transition from Humira
to a newer immunology franchise, with additional revenue in neuroscience,
oncology, and aesthetics.

**Q1 2026** net revenue was **$15.002 billion**, up **12.4%** reported and
**10.3%** operational. Immunology revenue was **$7.290 billion**: Skyrizi
**$4.483 billion**, Rinvoq **$2.119 billion**, Humira **$688 million**.
Neuroscience revenue was **$2.875 billion**, aesthetics **$1.186 billion**,
and oncology **$1.631 billion**, with Imbruvica down **24.7%**.

## Post-Humira replacement -- demonstrated, not merely projected

Skyrizi and Rinvoq's combined Q1 2026 revenue (**$6.602 billion**) is
several times Humira's own current residual run-rate (**$688 million**).
This is evidence that the immunology-franchise replacement AbbVie set out
to execute has **substantially already happened** in the reported quarter,
not a forward-looking projection this record is asked to take on faith.

## Pipeline conversion and business development

The FDA approved **Decnupaz** (pivekimab sunirine-pvzy) for blastic
plasmacytoid dendritic cell neoplasm (BPDCN), an ultra-rare hematologic
malignancy, in **May 2026** -- evidence that AbbVie's business-development
and internal-research pipeline is converting into approved products, not
solely revenue-stage assets. Q1 2026 acquired IPR&D and milestone expense
was **$744 million**, a real, recurring cost of this strategy.

## Thesis-break conditions

continue offsetting Humira and other franchise erosion, persistent
acquisition-related value destruction, or material deterioration in debt
and contingent-liability capacity. This is an analytical condition, not a
forecast.
