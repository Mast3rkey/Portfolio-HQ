# SNPS — Synopsys, Inc.

Last updated: 2026-09-07. This refresh directly reads Synopsys's SEC-filed
Q3 2026 results, Form 10-Q, and restructuring update. It replaces the prior
search-snippet evidence posture and establishes a dated pre–Investor Day
valuation baseline. It does not change the gate, target, holding, margin
policy, or any execution state.

## Decision summary

Synopsys reported strong Q3 growth after the Ansys acquisition, led by Design
Automation, but the quality of the reported earnings and the acquired balance
sheet require care. GAAP EPS included a large divestiture gain, management's
adjusted results exclude large acquisition-related costs, Design IP's
nine-month economics remain weaker, and the integration restructuring estimate
has increased.

The evidence supports continued monitoring, not gate clearance. The existing
gate explicitly requires review after the September 30, 2026 Investor Day and a
fresh normalized valuation model. That event has not occurred. The valuation
figures below are a transparent screen using management guidance, not a
normalized intrinsic-value conclusion.

## Verified reported facts

### Q3 2026 operations

- Q3 revenue was $2.477 billion versus $1.740 billion a year earlier, up 42%.
  Design Automation revenue was $2.003 billion, up 53%, and Design IP revenue
  was $473.9 million, up 11%. [SNPS-P01]
- Design Automation adjusted operating income was $905.0 million, a 45%
  adjusted margin. Design IP adjusted operating income was $125.4 million, a
  27% adjusted margin. These are management-defined non-GAAP segment measures.
  [SNPS-P01]
- For the first nine months, Design IP revenue was $1.335 billion versus $1.345
  billion, and adjusted operating income was $302.2 million versus $363.1
  million. The resulting adjusted margin was about 23% versus 27%. This longer
  window does not show the same strength as the single Q3 comparison. [SNPS-P01]
- GAAP operating income was $357.5 million, or a 14.4% margin. Non-GAAP
  operating margin was 41.6%. [SNPS-P01]

### EPS-quality screen

- Q3 GAAP net income was $545.8 million and diluted EPS was $2.84. Non-GAAP
  net income was $752.5 million and diluted EPS was $3.91. [SNPS-P01]
- GAAP earnings included a $425.4 million pre-tax gain on the Processor IP
  divestiture. The non-GAAP reconciliation adds back $402.4 million of acquired
  intangible amortization, $231.6 million of stock compensation, and $2.2
  million of restructuring charges; it removes $402.6 million of acquisition-
  and divestiture-related gains, and includes $26.9 million of tax adjustments.
  [SNPS-P01]
- The Processor IP business was sold to GlobalFoundries on June 1, 2026 for
  $443.3 million of cash consideration. Synopsys recorded the $425.4 million
  pre-tax gain plus $44.9 million of divestiture costs, for a $380.5 million net
  pre-tax gain. [SNPS-P02]

These bridges show why neither GAAP EPS nor management-adjusted EPS should be
used without qualification: the former contains a non-recurring gain, while
the latter excludes recurring acquisition-related amortization and substantial
stock compensation.

### Balance sheet and integration

- At July 31, 2026, cash and short-term investments totaled $3.608 billion.
  Short-term debt was $1.020 billion and long-term debt was $9.017 billion, for
  $10.037 billion of total debt and derived net debt of $6.430 billion.
  Quarterly interest expense was $133.2 million. [SNPS-P02]
- Goodwill was $26.835 billion and net intangible assets were $11.459 billion.
  Together they were $38.293 billion, or 80.2% of $47.726 billion of total
  assets. This is a derived balance-sheet concentration, not an impairment
  forecast. [SNPS-P02]
- On August 21, 2026, the board increased the expected restructuring cost from
  the previously disclosed $300-$350 million range to $425-$500 million. The
  plan is expected to continue through fiscal 2027, with the majority of the
  workforce reduction expected in fiscal 2026. [SNPS-P03]

## Management outlook

Synopsys's fiscal 2026 outlook calls for revenue of $9.690-$9.740 billion,
GAAP operating margin of approximately 10.4% at the midpoint, non-GAAP
operating margin of approximately 41.5%, GAAP EPS of $3.84-$4.08, non-GAAP EPS
of $15.04-$15.10, operating cash flow of approximately $2.8 billion, free cash
flow of approximately $2.6 billion, and capital expenditures of approximately
$225 million. The revenue outlook includes an expected $2.98 billion Ansys
contribution and divestiture effects. Management also assumes no further change
to applicable export-control or entity-list restrictions. These are issuer
forecasts, not observed results. [SNPS-P01]

## Material risks and open items

1. **Investor Day evidence is unavailable.** The official event is scheduled
   for September 30, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. ET. Its integration, margin, capital-
   allocation, and long-term framework must be assessed after disclosure.
2. **Adjusted earnings require a quality discount.** Large amortization, stock
   compensation, acquisition costs, and the divestiture gain make simple P/E
   comparisons unreliable.
3. **Design IP has not fully repaired.** Q3 improved, but nine-month revenue was
   roughly flat and adjusted operating income fell 17% year over year.
4. **Integration costs rose.** The restructuring range increased to
   $425-$500 million and the program extends through fiscal 2027.
5. **Balance-sheet concentration is high.** Goodwill and intangible assets are
   80.2% of total assets, and total debt remains $10.0 billion.
6. **Export-control exposure remains live.** Guidance assumes no further
   restriction changes; China and other controlled markets can alter access.
7. **Litigation is unresolved.** The 10-Q describes securities and derivative
   litigation connected to prior Design IP disclosures. No outcome is assumed.

## Investment thesis (descriptive, not a recommendation)

Synopsys combines a deeply embedded electronic-design-automation franchise
with Design IP and the newly acquired Ansys simulation portfolio. Q3 shows
strong Design Automation growth and high management-adjusted margins, but
Ansys integration, Design IP durability, leverage, earnings normalization, and
purchase-accounting exposure remain decision-relevant. The current evidence
supports monitoring through Investor Day, not a sleeve or weight change.

Evidence that could change the disposition:

- quantified long-term organic growth, margin, synergy, and deleveraging goals;
- a credible explanation of Design IP's nine-month economics and recovery path;
- a normalized earnings and cash-flow bridge that retains economically real
  stock compensation and acquisition costs; and
- a fresh valuation model after the September 30 disclosures.
