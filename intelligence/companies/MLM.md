## Business summary

Martin Marietta is a leading supplier of aggregates and other building
materials with operations across 28 U.S. states, Canada, and The Bahamas.
[MLM-C01] Per CLAUDE.md's existing Decisions Log (preserved here as
portfolio history, not independently re-verified by this record), Martin
Marietta absorbed Vulcan Materials' (VMC) aggregates-industry exposure
following VMC's full exit and consolidation into MLM on 2026-07-13, on the
reasoning that both companies shared the same infrastructure-spending/
housing/rate-cycle drivers and holding both was redundant sector exposure
rather than real diversification.

**Q1 2026 results show strong reported growth, with a mix-affected pricing
signal.** Revenue increased 17% to $1.362 billion and adjusted EBITDA
increased 14% to $364 million. [MLM-C02] Aggregates shipments increased
12% to 43.9 million tons, while reported average selling price was
roughly flat because of acquisition and geographic mix -- meaning this
specific quarter's pricing data point reflects portfolio mix shifts more
than a clean read on organic like-for-like pricing power. [MLM-C03]

**The company's acquisition program is active and expanding on multiple
fronts.** The February 2026 QUIKRETE asset exchange added operations
producing roughly 20 million tons annually and $450 million of cash while
transferring Martin Marietta's remaining cement and certain ready-mix
assets -- a portfolio-optimization move further concentrating the company
toward pure aggregates exposure. [MLM-C04] Separately, Martin Marietta
signed an agreement to acquire New Frontier Materials, an aggregates-led
business producing more than 8 million tons annually. [MLM-C05] At
2026-03-31, the company held $273 million of unrestricted cash and $1.2
billion of unused credit capacity, [MLM-C06] and management's 2026
guidance called for $7.0-$7.32 billion of revenue, $2.36-$2.50 billion of
adjusted EBITDA, and $550-$600 million of capital expenditures --
forward-looking guidance, not a realized result. [MLM-C07]

**Subsequent to the 2025 Form 10-K and the Q1 2026 results, on 2026-06-29,
Martin Marietta announced a $13.5 billion cash-and-stock transaction with
Lhoist North America to expand lime and limestone exposure.** [MLM-C08]
This transaction is large relative to Martin Marietta's own existing
scale and is a pending, unclosed transaction as of this record's evidence
cutoff -- it is not reflected in any of the financial figures cited above
and carries its own financing, integration, and execution risk.

**The evidence bundle's own explicit uncertainty judgment, carried forward
unresolved:** the investment case is exposed to construction cycles,
weather, energy and freight costs, permitting, reserve quality, acquisition
integration, and leverage from large transactions. [MLM-C09] This record
treats that as an open, unresolved condition, not a settled durable state.

## Investment thesis

**Concise statement:** Martin Marietta is a scale aggregates producer
showing strong reported revenue/EBITDA growth and real volume growth in
the most recently reported quarter, pursuing an increasingly aggressive
acquisition program culminating in a $13.5 billion pending Lhoist
transaction that is large relative to the company's own size and
introduces meaningful, currently unresolved integration and
capital-allocation risk.

**Expanded narrative:** Martin Marietta's core aggregates business showed
genuine strength in Q1 2026 -- double-digit revenue and EBITDA growth and
12% shipment-volume growth [MLM-C02, MLM-C03] -- while the company
simultaneously reshaped its portfolio through the QUIKRETE exchange
(shedding cement/ready-mix in favor of pure aggregates exposure) and moved
to acquire New Frontier Materials [MLM-C04, MLM-C05]. This is a company
actively using M&A to reshape its business, funded in part by disclosed
liquidity of $273 million cash and $1.2 billion unused credit capacity
[MLM-C06]. The subsequently announced $13.5 billion Lhoist transaction
[MLM-C08] is a materially larger step than the QUIKRETE exchange or New
Frontier agreement, and because it was announced after both the 2025 Form
10-K and the Q1 2026 results, this record's evidence base cannot assess
its financing structure, expected closing timeline, integration plan, or
pro forma leverage impact in any detail -- it is disclosed here as a
known, material, unresolved risk factor, not analyzed further than the
evidence bundle itself supports.

**Conditions required for the thesis to remain valid** (qualitative,
observable -- no arbitrary numerical threshold):
- Aggregates shipment-volume growth continues, or at minimum does not
  reverse sharply
- 2026 full-year guidance ($7.0-$7.32B revenue, $2.36-$2.50B adjusted
  EBITDA) is met or reaffirmed in subsequent quarters
- The Lhoist transaction's financing structure, once disclosed, does not
  impose leverage materially beyond what the company's existing liquidity
  and cash-flow generation can service
- Integration of QUIKRETE-exchanged operations and New Frontier Materials
  (once closed) proceeds without material disruption

**Thesis-weakening conditions** (analytical judgment, presented for
review -- observable and qualitative, deliberately without a specific
numerical trigger):
- A sustained reversal of aggregates shipment-volume growth
- A withdrawal or material downward revision of 2026 full-year guidance
- Disclosure that the Lhoist transaction's financing materially increases
  balance-sheet leverage beyond a level the company's cash flow can
  comfortably service
- Evidence of integration difficulty across the QUIKRETE, New Frontier, or
  (once closed) Lhoist transactions
